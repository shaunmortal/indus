## Numerals: what the literature says
- Parpola: short strokes = units (up to 9, 1-2 tiers), inverted semicircles = tens; beyond that uncertain. Rejects Wells' volumetric readings.
- Wells: long stroke = 10, short = 1, "V" sign = measure; 3 vessels with 3/6/7 long strokes measured 27.3/55.6/65.9 L (~9.24 L per stroke). Never replicated (n=3).
- Mukhopadhyay 2019: 22 stroke signs = NUM; numeral PRECEDES counted sign; strokes precede CROP/sheaf signs ~100x; 8 two-sign sets where only stroke count varies; strokes = metrological denominations whose value varies by commodity (proto-cuneiform analogy). 2023: tablet reverses = license fees.
- Kak 1988/1990 (fringe): U=5, fish=10.
- Pettersson 1999 Indus numerals on metal tools, IJHS 34(2).
- Consensus: numeral first; strokes = ones; tens = semicircles (mainstream) vs long strokes (Wells). Base unresolved.

## Accounting objects
- Frenez & Tosi 2005 Lothal sealings (~70-100 from one warehouse square; backs = bags, boxes, jars, door pegs). Frenez 2023: local goods management.
- Rao 2018: Harappa 1997 three-sided tablets (22 identical) = ration records (proto-Elamite analogy).
- Kanmer passport tablets (see india_finds).
- NO paper matches tablet numerals to weight denominations.

## Mesopotamian side
- Dilmun mina ~1370 g = 100 x 13.7 g (Bibby 1970; UET V 796; Roaf 1982 Iraq 44). Zaccagnini 1986 Iraq 48: 28 Harappan-type weights.
- Laursen & Steinkeller 2017, Appendix 1 = all Ur III/Isin Gulf-trade texts (print). Not mined for Meluhha quantities.
- Ratnagar 2003 Paléorient 29/1 (open on Persée): Indus weights abroad few and small.
