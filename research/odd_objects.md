# Three "odd" objects checked (agent report, 2026-10-15)

## 1. Lothal L-56 terracotta seal (Frenez 2023 fig. 12D "cuneiform-type wedges") — INDUS-ONLY
Frenez 2018 ("Private Person or Public Persona?", Walking with the Unicorn, fig. 10): "terracotta stamp seal L-56 with Indus signs composed in part of wedges made using a flexible stylus". Technological observation (stylus in wet clay), NOT cuneiform. Verdict: Indus-only. Check CISI vol.1 entry L-56 (we have the PDF).

## 2. Susa "text O", Louvre Sb 9382 — UNIDENTIFIED / aberrant Linear Elamite
- Louvre: https://collections.louvre.fr/en/ark:/53355/cl010182134 — MDP XXVI "O", terracotta 11.5x7.5x3.8 cm, de Mecquenem; dated c.2120-2100 BCE (Puzur-Inshushinak); script "élamite linéaire syllabique", noted "slightly different"; on display Richelieu 231; 5 zoomable images downloadable after terms.
- Hatamti doc 15: 43 signs, 8 lines, writing type unknown; only ri, ni identified; no Indus mention. Meriggi 1971 excluded it; older lit: "different, probably older writing system".
- Fuls 2024 IJAS abstract does not mention it; the "Indus-like graphemes" claim is unverified; date conflicts (2100 vs "early 2nd mill.").
- Plate PDF: https://center-for-decipherment.ch/tool/plates/linear-elam/o.pdf
=> LEAD (computational, laptop-feasible): download the 5 Louvre images + plate; compare the 43 signs shape-by-shape against the 418 Mahadevan drawings (we have them) and the Linear Elamite sign list. If >5 signs match Indus forms beyond chance, text O becomes the only candidate mixed object. Low prior, but cheap.

## 3. Richter-Ushanas "A bilingual from Failaka" (2005) — UNIDENTIFIED, fringe
Reads a Failaka seal "logophonetically" via the Rg-Veda. No Kjærum/museum number given anywhere. Likely candidates: Kjærum 1983 nos. 279 and 319 (Dilmun seals with "(pseudo-?)inscriptions in Indus characters", Laursen 2010) — Indus-only. His "Ur bilingual" = BM 1928,1009.56 = Gadd no.1, cuneiform only. Non-evidence.
