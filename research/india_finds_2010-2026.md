# New inscribed Indus objects from India/Pakistan, 2010-2026 (agent report, 2026-10-15; web-only, harappa.com & academia.edu blocked -> several items UNVERIFIED)

## Hard answers to the five flags
(a) Sealing with container contents known: NONE. Closest: Shikarpur thread/knot sealings (package sealings, contents unstated).
(b) Inscribed weight with quantity: NONE anywhere.
(c) New inscription >12 signs: NONE. Dholavira signboard = 10.
(d) Any bilingual / other script: NONE.
(e) Same seal impression at two sites: NONE. Kanmer: ONE seal impressed on 3 tablets at one site, each tablet with a DIFFERENT incised text on the back.

## Most useful leads
- **Dholavira, Bisht 2015 final report (ASI Memoir, ~940 pp).** PDF circulates: https://ancientportsantiques.com/wp-content/uploads/Documents/PLACES/IndOc-Gulf/Dholavira-Bisht2015.pdf . Claimed ~150 inscriptions, "scores published for the first time" (unverified). UNESCO dossier seal inventory pp.121-154: https://whc.unesco.org/document/184545 . -> Likely largest block of texts NOT in M77/ICIT. Candidate HOLD-OUT / prediction set.
- **Kanmer "passport" tablets** (Kharakwal et al. 2012 p.487; Agrawal et al. Antiquity 84 Project Gallery 2010): same seal, three different incised inscriptions. Structural anchor: seal text = fixed identity; incised text = variable (date? load? destination?). Test variable part for numerals.
- **Shikarpur** (MSU Baroda 2008-09): sealing with THREE consecutive stampings, thread/knot on reverse. PDF via harappa.com (browser needed).
- **Farmana** seals: Uesugi & Jamison 2020, Heritage 8.1, open PDF https://www.heritageuniversityofkerala.com/JournalPDF/Volume8.1/1.pdf
- **Bhirrana** seal: Uesugi et al. 2016, Heritage 4, https://www.heritageuniversityofkerala.com/JournalPDF/Volume4/1.pdf
- **Bagasra/Gola Dhoro**: 7 unicorn seals + sealings (Jamison et al., prob. Heritage 5, 2017).
- **Binjor/4MSR, Karanpura, Khirsara, Kotada Bhadli 2025, Rakhigarhi 2022-24**: seals/sealings reported in news/abstracts only; no sign drawings online. Rakhigarhi cylinder seal (5 signs + crocodile) unpublished by excavators.
- Pakistan: HARP material all in CISI 3.1 (2010). Lakhan-jo-daro: 3 unfinished seals (workshop). Nothing new from Mohenjo-daro.

## Kanmer follow-up (2026-10-15)
- Antiquity Project Gallery page (antiquity.ac.uk/projgall/kharakwal324/) is 404. Primary: Agrawal, Kharakwal, Osada et al. 2010, "Redefining the Harappan hinterland", Antiquity 84(323) Project Gallery. First-season report: Kharakwal, Rawat & Osada, "Kanmer: a Harappan site in Kachchh", in Indus Civilization: Text and Context vol. II (Manohar).
- Per arXiv 1204.3800 (Kalyanaraman; fringe author, but quoting the gallery note): the three ~2 cm tablets carry the same seal impression on one face and incised marks on the other that "cannot be clearly matched to known script glyphs"; possibly personal/workshop marks. => weakens the "fixed identity + variable text" lead; the variable side may not be script at all. Need the original photos (Kharakwal et al. 2012 report p.487).
- FOUND primary: Agrawal, Kharakwal, Rawat, Osada & Goyal 2010, "Redefining the Harappan hinterland", Antiquity 84(323) Project Gallery, DOI 10.15184/S2978855200902828 (Cambridge Core reader: https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/11D7D7DE4161A3DDB82755A7856D5521/core-reader). Three clay sealings with central hole, unicorn motif, "different motifs" on top view. Parpola: anthropomorphic sign (grass skirt, upturned feet) otherwise only on 10 Mohenjo-daro objects. Yadav: M38 on the three Kanmer impressions occurs 6x at Mohenjo-daro in M77; red-ware sherd sign ~ M33 (once at Harappa). Yadav reads the impressions as cargo stamping. => Kanmer-Mohenjo-daro shared sign-string: a cross-site identity trail, not an anchor.
