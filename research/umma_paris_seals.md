# Umma 2017 seal and the Paris cuneiform seal (agent report, 2026-10-15)

## Umma (Tell Jokha), SAHI 2017 — NOT an anchor
- Hulínek & Hulínková Ťuchová (eds) 2018, Preliminary Report SAHI Tell Jokha 2017 (Season 2), Bratislava, ISBN 978-80-89704-05-7. No PDF online (sahi.sk empty to fetchers; ResearchGate 326752819 behind bot check). Print: STU Bratislava, SVK Košice. Cuneiform section: Mittermayer pp. 62-63.
- Frenez 2023 fig. 3D = this seal (academia.edu/108938835; figure viewed): fragment of square grey steatite seal, unicorn head, TWO Indus signs preserved (oval with trident-like element; U/jar-type), rest broken. No cuneiform.
- Find code TJ17-PRO-S51 => "PRO" probably prospection/survey => likely SURFACE find, no stratigraphy, no associated tablets (inference, unverified). IAA page says Harappan material came from topographic survey.
- Later: Hulínek & Hulínková Ťuchová 2023 in Rollinger et al. (eds), CRAI 64/Melammu 12, ÖAW, pp. 849-868 (paywalled) may give context.

## Paris (BnF Cabinet des Médailles) chlorite seal — NOT bilingual
- Vidale 2005 "The Short-Horned Bull on the Indus Seals", South Asian Archaeology 2003: 147-158, OPEN PDF https://publications.dainst.org/books/dai/catalog/view/2043/2440/4491 . Only TWO gaur seals with cuneiform exist: BM 120573 and this one. Bought on Beirut antiquities market; presented by Jarrige at an SAA conference; unpublished.
- Reading (Glassner 2002: 361 n.215): "Ur.4 Nin.Ildum dumu Ur.gi7" ~ "servant of Nin-ildum, son of Ur-gi" — Sumerian/Amorite milieu.
- Frenez 2023 fig. 3F viewed: square chlorite, two ruled lines of cuneiform (~3+6 signs), short-horned bull below. NO Indus signs.
- Planned study "Hybrid Identities: Seals with Harappan Motifs and Cuneiform Inscriptions" (Frenez, Marchesi, Vidale, SAA Stockholm 2014) apparently never published.
- Unknown: BnF inventory number; Marchesi's reading.

## Other
- Ligabue "Persian Gulf" seal: Indus bull + 3 Linear Elamite signs za-ši-ri (Hatamti doc 22; CDLI P247651). Unprovenanced. No Indus signs.
- **Lothal terracotta seal with two Indus signs partly formed of cuneiform-type wedges** (Frenez 2023 fig. 12D). Curious; check CISI for the object.
- Dilmun seals with cuneiform legends (Kjærum 1983): no Indus signs.

**Bottom line: no object anywhere carries both Indus signs and readable script.**
