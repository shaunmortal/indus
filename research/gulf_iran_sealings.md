# Sealed documents / mixed-script objects outside the Indus (agent report, 2026-10-15; web-only)

**Bottom line:** No published tablet, bulla, envelope or tag anywhere carries an impression of an Indus-sign seal together with cuneiform/Linear Elamite or a count of goods. No excavated object combines Indus signs with another script except eroded Susa text O (Sb 9382; Hatamti DB doc 15: 43 signs, only "ri","ni" read).

## By site
- Failaka: Kjærum 1983 (~400 stamp seals, some Gulf-type with Indus signs); Glassner 1984 cuneiform (Persée, open: https://www.persee.fr/doc/mom_0766-0510_1984_rpm_9_1_2040); newer Marchesi edition in Failaka/Dilmun vol. 6. No Indus-sign sealing. FRINGE: Richter-Ushanas 2005 "A bilingual from Failaka with Sumerian and Indus signs" (self-published) — object never identified; non-evidence until located in Kjærum.
- Bahrain: Saar (Crawford 2001; 261 sealing fragments, package/bale/jar sealings) — no Indus signs reported; archive.org PDF blocked. Qala'at al-Bahrain: Parpola 1994 "Harappan inscriptions: analytical catalogue of the Indus inscriptions from the Near East" in Højlund & Andersen QAB vol.1: 304-315 = THE authoritative checklist (not OA). 2026 JNES 85: three clay tokens stamped with cuneiform-legend seals (Yagli'el servant of Panipa) — cuneiform only, but proves sealed tokens c.1700 BC.
- Oman/UAE: Salut ST1 jar sherd stamped with Indus-script seal (Frenez et al. 2016 PSAS 46, open: https://www.archaeopresspublishing.com/ojs/index.php/PSAS/article/view/1247) — sign-string on a commodity container, no text/count. Ras al-Jinz RJ-2 inscribed sherds (Cleuziou et al. 1994). Tell Abraq Gulf seal.
- Iran: Susa Sb 5614 Gulf-type seal buffalo + Indus signs (Louvre). Konar Sandal: Linear Elamite tablets + cylinder seal with Indus iconography, no script; Pittman 2013 "Seals and Sealings from Konar Sandal South" = place to check for Indus-type impressions (not OA). Tepe Yahya, Shahr-i Sokhta, Shahdad: nothing mixed.
- Central Asia: Gonur (elephant seal, Exc.9 Room 19), Altyn Depe, Shortugai (rhino seal, Francfort 1989 pl.75.7). Frenez & Vidale 2019 catalogue all. No bridge.
- Desset 2022 / Fuls 2024 (OA: https://ijas.usb.ac.ir/article_8463.html): no mixed-script object.
