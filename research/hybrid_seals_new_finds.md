# Hybrid seals in collections + new extra-Indus finds 2018-2026 (agent report, 2026-10-15)

## A. Hybrid (Harappan motif + cuneiform) in collections
- Frenez-Marchesi-Vidale "Hybrid Identities" (SAA 2014): proceedings = South Asian Archaeology and Art 2014 (Dev Publishers 2020); title absent from all three authors' lists -> apparently never published; content likely in Frenez 2023 fig. 3.
- BM 122947 (U 16220, CDLI P541065): Ur cylinder, Indus-influenced motifs, NO signs, NO text.
- BM 123208: Gulf-type round seal, pseudo-inscription, no cuneiform. BM 122187, BM 120228: Indus/round seals, signs only.
- Penn 31-43-74 / 31-43-76: Ur round button seals, doubtful signs, no cuneiform.
- YBC Gungunum yr 10 tablet (Hallo & Buchanan 1965): Gulf seal impression on dated cuneiform tablet; paragorth: pictorial, no Indus signs. Buchanan 1981 catalogue PDF: https://babylonian-collection.yale.edu/sites/default/files/files/Buchanan1981.pdf (could confirm).
- CDLI P537730 = BM 120573 (known). Only CDLI record with an "Indus" remark.
- Louvre, Met, ISAC, Morgan, Ashmolean, Iraq Museum, Ligabue, auction houses: nothing with Indus signs + cuneiform.

## B. New finds outside the Indus, 2018-2026
- Bahrain tokens (JNES 85, 2026): 3 clay tokens (one tetrahedral, "nearly identical to Kanmer tokens") stamped with seals carrying cuneiform legends (Yagli-el servant of Panipa), c.1700 BC, unstratified. Cuneiform only. Closest "Indus-style admin + cuneiform" object.
- Dahwa DH7, Oman (2022): silver seal-ring with Indus bison, motif only.
- Abraq 2 UAQ (PAM 33, 2024): Dilmun-related seal, no signs.
- Failaka F6 2025 (Kuwaiti-Danish): "seals" in temple, press only, unverified.
- Girsu 2024-25 (BM): 200+ Akkadian tablets, 60 sealings, no Indus material reported.
- Shahr-i Sokhta, Konar Sandal (resumed 2023), Al-Khashbah, Bat, Ras al-Hadd, Ur 2015-23, Saudi, Maldives: nothing Indus-inscribed.

## Verdict
No object combining Indus SIGNS with cuneiform beyond BM 120573-type (which has no signs). No new Indus-inscribed object abroad 2018-2026.
