# Draft request: photograph of Ur seal-impression U.17355 (Legrain 1951, UE X, Cat. 623)

To: the curator of the Near East section, Penn Museum (if the object is in Philadelphia) / Department of the Middle East, British Museum (if in London). Legrain's catalogue gives no museum number for Cat. 623; the 1930-31 division of finds decides which.

Subject: Photograph request, Ur field number U.17355 (Legrain, Ur Excavations X, 1951, no. 623)

Dear Curator,

I am an independent researcher in India working on the Indus script with a fully open, reproducible dataset (github.com/shaunmortal/indus). In Legrain's Ur Excavations X (1951) catalogue, no. 623 is listed only as "Monkey and scorpion (?). U. 17355" with a small plate photograph (plate 36) and no description of shape, material or back. The impression is square and the device is unclear; it is the one object in the volume that cannot be classified from the publication as either a Mesopotamian stamp seal or a seal of Indus type.

Could you tell me whether U.17355 is in your collection and, if so, let me have a photograph of the face and the back, with its dimensions and material? I would cite the museum and the photographer in any publication, and I am happy to pay the usual reproduction fee.

With thanks,
Ayush Kumar
